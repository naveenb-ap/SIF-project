What is a SIF?

A Specialised Investment Fund is run by a mutual fund house under SEBI's mutual fund rules, with a separate SIF brand. The main difference: it can take short positions of up to 25% of its money through derivatives. Minimum investment is ₹10 lakh per investor.

How we explain it to clients

A hybrid SIF carries an umbrella

Most of the money earns steadily in arbitrage and bonds, some sits in shares, and a small "short" part gains when markets fall. On a rainy day you still get a little wet, just less.

In March 2026 the Nifty fell 11.3%. On ₹10 lakh, the middle hybrid SIF lost ₹19,200. A balanced advantage fund lost ₹66,900. The Nifty 50 lost ₹1,13,000.

How SIFs came about

SEBI created SIFs for the gap between a ₹500 mutual fund SIP and a ₹50 lakh PMS.

Before 2025

The gap

MFs from a few hundred rupees, PMS at ₹50 lakh, AIFs at ₹1 crore. Nothing in between.

Jul 2024

SEBI proposes

A consultation paper suggests a new product run by fund houses, from ₹10 lakh.

Dec 2024

Approved

SEBI's board approves the SIF framework.

27 Feb 2025

The rulebook

₹10 lakh minimum, shorts up to 25%, separate SIF brand, risk band 1–5.

8 Oct 2025

First SIF live

The first strategy publishes its NAV. Hybrids follow within three weeks.

Jul 2026

One licence

NISM Series V-D covers both mutual funds and SIFs.

FAQs

Who can invest in a SIF?

Resident and eligible investors who put in at least ₹10 lakh across a fund house's SIF strategies, subject to SEBI rules and each strategy's documents. Accredited investors may have different rules.

What do I need to distribute SIFs?

A valid ARN and the NISM Series V-D certification (MF and SIF distributors), plus empanelment with the fund house. AssetPlus runs free live training for V-D starting 16 Nov 2026.

How is a SIF taxed?

SIF units are taxed like mutual fund units. The rate depends on the strategy's tax category and on how long the client holds. For an equity-taxed strategy, gains after 12 months are taxed at 12.5% with a ₹1.25 lakh yearly exemption; before 12 months, at 20%. Other strategies are taxed at the client's slab rate. One month can change the bill.

Can clients take money out any day?

Not always. Many hybrid and asset-allocator SIFs are interval funds that redeem only on set days, with notice periods. Check each strategy's redemption schedule before investing.

How is a SIF different from PMS?

A SIF is a pooled fund under the mutual fund rules, from ₹10 lakh, with daily NAV and tax only when units are sold. PMS starts at ₹50 lakh, holds securities in the client's own account and is taxed on each trade.
